# Licensing and permitted use

Project-authored software and documentation use the **PolyForm Noncommercial
License 1.0.0**, except material with its own explicit licence or a preserved
earlier grant. The SPDX identifier is `PolyForm-Noncommercial-1.0.0`.
The complete, unmodified terms are in the
[repository LICENSE](https://github.com/Tknecht4/open_agronomy_agent/blob/main/LICENSE)
and the [official licence text](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/noncommercial/1.0.0).
Project attribution and the required notice are in
[NOTICE](https://github.com/Tknecht4/open_agronomy_agent/blob/main/NOTICE).
The example company in the standard licence text is illustrative.

The licence permits non-commercial purposes, including research and evaluation.
It also permits use by the charitable, educational, public research, public
safety or health, environmental protection, and government institutions it
lists, regardless of funding source or obligations. It is broader than a
research-only licence. Commercial use is not generally granted; any use must
fall within the standard licence's permitted purposes or have separate
permission from the relevant rights holders. This summary does not change
the licence terms. The project is source-available, rather than OSI open source.

## Earlier Apache grants

Earlier versions offered under Apache-2.0 retain those grants, including
commercial-use permissions, when their conditions are met. The earlier text
is preserved in
[LICENSES/Apache-2.0.txt](https://github.com/Tknecht4/open_agronomy_agent/blob/main/LICENSES/Apache-2.0.txt).
It is not an alternative licence for new PolyForm-licensed contributions.

Checksum-bound corpus releases, graph manifests, historical research and
evaluation receipts, and source registries retain their original licences and
identities. In particular, the curated seed graph and frozen
production-foundations v1/v2 text retain their earlier Apache grant.
Historical source-registry descriptions do not override the current software
licence or grant rights beyond a source's own terms.

## Separate data, model, and dependency terms

Third-party data, publications, models, dependencies, map services, and private
uploads retain their own terms. The project licence does not relicense them.
Attribution, non-commercial, share-alike, access, and redistribution conditions
apply independently. For example, identified UBC-derived research material
retains CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; PolyForm's institutional permissions do not override
that material's restrictions. Unknown rights or public availability are not
permission to redistribute. Previously excluded sources remain excluded.

Model configuration `license` fields describe upstream weights, so their
existing Apache-2.0 declarations remain. Consult the
[third-party notices](https://github.com/Tknecht4/open_agronomy_agent/blob/main/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md)
and each source's recorded terms. Review of compiled native app distributions
is separate from this source-repository licensing change.
