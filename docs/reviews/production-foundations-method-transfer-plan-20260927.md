# Production foundations method transfer: candidate plan

Status: development candidate, not an active corpus or agronomic-performance claim. Reference main: `0a942a3`. The v1 nine-card release, prior 14-case transfer audit and eight-case topic confirmation remain frozen evidence.

## Observation, model, interpretation

- **Observation:** Seven of the ten prior expected-card misses were already absent at query fit: four jurisdiction, two topic and one lexical exclusion. A broad economics-topic selector failed the separate confirmation cohort and was removed.
- **Model:** Publisher geography was being used as method applicability. A project-authored, source-supported METHOD row can declare a reviewed US/Canada reasoning scope while retaining each source's original jurisdiction and forbidding field or regulatory authority.
- **Interpretation to test:** Typed method obligations and country-level method retrieval should deliver relevant context across cases without transferring local targets, current values, label permissions or field facts. Delivery alone will not establish answer benefit.

## Candidate and rivals

Seven v2 method rows cover seed-count conversion, thermal time, partial and enterprise budgeting, liquidity, seasonal cash flow and nutrient-plan input organization. Their source-support receipt binds claim IDs to US/Canadian source locators and snapshot hashes. The active profile is unchanged; a separate profile admits only these rows under `context_only` and the hash-bound candidate policy.

Rivals retained: weak source content, lexical classifier misses, displacement of stronger context, model failure to use an admitted card, verifier/fallback erasing a useful draft, generation variability, and independently helpful calculator effects. All are checked as separate stages.

### Stop and acceptance rules set before inspecting v2 answers

1. Model-free: all nine positive cases must have the relevant METHOD row in final selected context; none of the three distractors may receive a METHOD row. Missing or forged scope, unknown target country and unadmitted corpus must fail closed.
2. Authority: every selected METHOD capsule must be `field_action_authority=not_authorized`; METHOD coverage cannot satisfy field observation, local nutrient rate, current price or regulated-label obligations.
3. Answer benefit: at least three more complete positive answers than **each** comparator, no new unsafe answer in any of the 16 cases, no lost complete control answer, and no increased fallback count. Any identity mismatch, incomplete run or new material error stops promotion consideration.
4. Follow with a source-distinct, unopened confirmation cohort and order-reversed discriminating reruns for uncertain pairs. The exposed cohort can identify defects but cannot alone qualify generalized efficacy.

The implementation, focused tests, corpus/runtime checks, public-package build, full backend/frontend/docs gates, and a fresh independent review are required before a reviewable PR. A passing PR does not itself approve activation of the candidate profile.

## Observed first comparison and declared repair (post hoc)

The first v2 method candidate delivered a relevant row to all nine positive cases, yet blind grades were **3 complete / 8 partial / 5 unsafe**, versus active **3 / 10 / 3** and original nine-card candidate **3 / 9 / 4**. Four active fallbacks, four original fallbacks and four v2 fallbacks occurred. This fails the answer-benefit and safety rules above. The v2 candidate remains non-active; the result is retained, not rewritten as a success.

Two wrong-jurisdiction fallbacks exposed a shared destination-parser defect: mixed US/Canadian questions were assigned the cited source's province instead of the user's field state. Separately, the small model misstated total cost, called working capital a ratio and treated financing proceeds as cash outflow while a correct method card was in context. The repair therefore has two distinct parts: resolve operation-site geography from the question, and route a narrow explanation-only request to project-authored source-supported METHOD text with an explicit deterministic-renderer receipt. Numeric calculations, adoption decisions, pesticide uses and local rates stay on the existing tool/model/authority path. This is a method skill intervention, **not** evidence that the model learned the material from context.

The first 16 cases are now development diagnostics. A second cohort was authored without inspecting implementation or model outputs and saved unopened at `data/eval/production_foundations_method_confirmation_v2.jsonl`, SHA-256 `3d24cf4e67369d2e6031c697196bf24a68b2a67fd94eb4b56e9d21b866bc6bf5`. Its source/rubric companion is SHA-256 `34b72439f5965920218b989c98f4163bbf29038cd64d6c7bf365d1f4e15c48ef`. It has seven positive method cases spanning all seven families, three safety controls and two distractors. Its author reported twelve directly opened sources and no exact URL overlap with the first cohort or runtime source registries; one source later had a transient fetch failure. The case content must remain unopened until the repaired candidate and comparator identities are frozen.

For the repaired intervention, require zero new unsafe answers against same-code active and original-candidate comparators, at least three extra complete positives on the exposed development cohort, no complete-control loss, and no false method bypass on safety or distractor controls. The unopened confirmation then decides generalization; a failure there blocks activation and any efficacy claim regardless of development gains.

The source-supported repair reached **12 complete / 4 partial / 0 unsafe** in a second blind grade of the exposed 16 cases: all nine transfer positives and all three distractors were complete; four safety controls were partial. Same-code active was **3 / 11 / 2**, and original nine-card candidate **3 / 10 / 3**. This is a development result and includes nine deterministic method responses, six model drafts, one deterministic regulated hold and one verifier fallback. It does not show that the model itself learned the methods. A final guard excludes pesticide/label mixed requests from the method renderer, with focused regression coverage; confirmation and repository gates remain outstanding.

Independent implementation review subsequently found that this version still allowed unsupported operation sites and mixed decision requests into the deterministic renderer. These are required-boundary violations, so the 12/16 result is **historical pre-repair development evidence**, not a qualified result for the repaired implementation. The first 12-case confirmation executed on that rejected identity and is retained as invalidated evidence; its answers cannot qualify the repair. The next confirmation must be independently authored after the safety repair and a rebase to current main.

Before opening the 12-case confirmation, require: all seven method families represented by a selected, packed METHOD card on their positive case; no METHOD renderer on the five safety/distractor controls; at least five of seven positive answers complete and at least three more complete positives than each comparator; zero new unsafe answers and no lost complete control. Grade blinded against the second cohort's frozen rubric. A failure blocks activation consideration; subsequent repairs would require a *new* independent cohort.

## Source-distinct v3 holdout and disposition

Independent implementation review accepted the appendix version at commit `fe2426e` for a **non-active review PR**, after repairs to mixed-request and foreign-site boundaries. That review was a code and product-path boundary check, not an efficacy result. The reviewed identity and preregistered gate were frozen before opening the new 12-case cohort in [the final freeze](artifacts/production-foundations-method-holdout-freeze-reviewed-20260928.json).

The first execution failed before scoring because the author used `id` and the runner expected `case_id`. An execution copy added `case_id = id` to each row without changing question or rubric content. The original cohort remains unchanged. The same pinned Gemma 4 E2B revision and 320-token limit then ran both arms through the product execution core. The independent grader saw answer labels without the active/candidate key and checked 24 answers with exact quote spans. [The decoded record](artifacts/production-foundations-method-holdout-v3-result-20260928.json) retains answer text, grades, hashes, fallbacks, and run identity.

The candidate delivered the relevant METHOD row to final context on all seven positive cases, with none on five controls. The model still handled all seven positives. Yet blind grades were **active: 0 complete, 11 partial, 1 unsafe; candidate: 1 complete, 7 partial, 4 unsafe**. Three new unsafe answers were in enterprise budgeting, liquidity, and cash flow (PFMH3-04/05/06). The candidate's appendix did not retract incorrect model claims about contribution margin, the balance sheet, or net cash surplus. Both arms shared the PFMH3-12 mulch-mound error. Thus the preregistered complete-answer and zero-new-unsafe gates failed, even though retrieval transfer succeeded.

The automatic answer appendix was removed from the product path and candidate profile. The source-linked v2 corpus, typed method obligations, provenance, jurisdiction fit, and bounded context remain as a non-active research candidate. The v3 cohort is now exposed; further intervention must be assessed on a new source-distinct holdout. Exact replay of all external source bytes is limited because source snapshot hashes and locators were retained without redistributing those bytes. Local disk exhaustion interrupted the prior full Python rerun, so that attempt is not a pass.

A post-removal ablation attempt on these exposed cases is **invalid**: the pinned local model snapshot was unavailable to the loader, so zero of twelve model generations completed. Its fallbacks cannot estimate the answer quality of the context-only candidate. No automatic download or substitute model was used.
