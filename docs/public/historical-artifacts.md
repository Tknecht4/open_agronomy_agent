## Promotion rule

Archive assets move only through a dated successor admission: source and reuse
rights, provenance, jurisdiction, privacy, duplicate disposition, extraction
quality, policy role, and reproducible rebuild must all be recorded. A file's
presence in an old checkout never makes it model-visible.
