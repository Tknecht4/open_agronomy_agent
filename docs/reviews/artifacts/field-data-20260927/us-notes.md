# U.S. field-data source notes — 2026-09-27 UTC

Research-only inventory: [us-sources.json](us-sources.json) contains 23 records with source URLs, exact search queries, rights status, and raw-sample receipts. These are **record candidates**, not 23 independent field populations. Bushland crop records share the same station, the two Fort Collins GRACEnet studies share a site, KBS records share a research farm, and the GRACEnet network archive can contain listed site records. The catalog record for DRIVES exposes metadata, while the measurement tables require a request. No file was activated for retrieval, training, or a benchmark.

## First candidates for distinct field-question tasks

- [Midwest corn stalk nitrate survey](https://agdatacommons.nal.usda.gov/articles/dataset/Data_from_Late-season_corn_stalk_nitrate_measurements_across_the_US_Midwest_from_2006_to_2018/24668283): a genuine broad field-level cohort, 10,675 fields and 32,025 stalk measurements in 2006–2018 across six states. The [source license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) is CC BY 4.0. The full principal CSV was inspected. It supports N-status, input-history, missingness, and scope questions. The publisher explicitly says **yield is absent**, management data are inconsistent, and high manure N rates may be erroneous. Never ask it to prove yield response or treat county centroids as precise field locations.
- [Akron, Colorado, spatial yield](https://agdatacommons.nal.usda.gov/articles/dataset/Data_from_Topographic_position_index_predicts_within-field_yield_variation_in_a_dryland_cereal_production_system/28914434): source-specific [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) and a small inspectable CSV. It links 2019–2022 crop yield, 30 m soil sample points, topography, N and seasonal weather over 18 management units. The authors state that yield-monitor values were gridded to 5 m and extracted at soil locations, so rows are spatially dependent and repeated years at a location are not independent cases. The inspected first two wheat yields differ sharply (363.83 and 4.79 kg/ha), a prompt for data-quality questions rather than an assumed treatment effect.
- [Transforming Drainage](https://agdatacommons.nal.usda.gov/articles/dataset/Transforming_Drainage_Research_Data_USDA-NIFA_Award_No_2015-68007-23193_/24665985): CC BY 4.0 plot/field data with 39 experiments, paired practices, yield, soil, water, weather, and detailed management tables. The publisher's site-specific protocol and missing-data caveats are essential. Metadata and file list were verified; no file downloaded in this pass.
- [Dryad Midwest N-response](https://datadryad.org/dataset/doi:10.5061/dryad.66t1g1k2g): 49 corn site-years from eight states, 16 N rates, four replicates, soil and weather workbooks, observed yield, and plot boundaries. The exact DOI's [DataCite rights metadata](https://api.datacite.org/dois/10.5061/dryad.66t1g1k2g) was independently checked and reports CC0-1.0; its snapshot hash is in the source ledger. The landing page lists specific experimental anomalies (hail residue, ponding, an erroneous 45 kg N/ha application, and lodging). Keep these in evaluation cases and distinguish reported economic-optimal N (derived) from measured yield.
- [Morrow Plots v2](https://databank.illinois.edu/datasets/IDB-3676612): planting, treatment, and yield since 1888 with a specific `CC BY` license on the Illinois Data Bank record. The direct page fetch returned HTTP 403 through the research browser, while the indexed source result exposed the metadata and license. The exact CC BY version and file bytes should be checked before reuse. Its one-site long time series must split by plot/period with awareness of historical changes.

## Rights and access boundaries

- [KBS LTER MCSE yield table](https://lter.kbs.msu.edu/datatables/51) and [Biodiversity Gradient yield table](https://lter.kbs.msu.edu/datatables/75) explicitly state that core database data may not be published without written permission of the lead investigator or project director. Their download buttons do not confer benchmark redistribution rights. The Resource Gradient experiment is the same KBS core-policy family.
- [DRIVES catalog](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/diverse-rotations-improve-valuable-ecosystem-services-drives-database) says only metadata/inventory files are openly linked; substantive yield/management tables require a data-request form, emailed database access, and acknowledgment for resulting products. Canadian sites have a separate request. Do not infer a blanket grant from `accessLevel: public` on catalog metadata.
- [OFPEDATA](https://github.com/paulhegedus/OFPEDATA) contains real farmers' yield, grain protein, as-applied inputs, and field boundary files, including one Manitoba farm. Its [MIT license text](https://github.com/paulhegedus/OFPEDATA/blob/master/LICENSE.md) calls the subject “software and associated documentation”; no explicit farm-measurement or geometry license was located. Keep data reuse rights `unknown` even though the repository is public. It may be useful after a source-specific rights confirmation, especially for upload-format testing.
- [Magruder](https://www.glten.org/experiments/32) links a public Oklahoma State XLSX, but GLTEN explicitly records the data license and policy as unknown. The experiment's 1947 relocation of surface soil also requires a time-series break. For Sanborn Field, the [Missouri experiment-station page](https://moaes.missouri.edu/central-missouri-research-extension-and-education-center/sanborn-field/) and agronomic reports were found, but no sufficiently verified downloadable long-form, licensed record was found; it is not counted.
- The [GRACEnet catalog archive](https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/gracenet-greenhouse-gas-reduction-through-agricultural-carbon-enhancement-network) lists downloadable templates and a multi-site archive. Its public-access label is not a site-wide data-rights statement. Individual Ag Data Commons site records in the JSON have explicit licenses and are safer rights anchors. The [LTAR access page](https://ltar.ars.usda.gov/data/data-access/) is a portal/index, not an extra independent field dataset.

The dataset's weather companion is about 218 MB and was deliberately not downloaded.

## Benchmark and ingestion implications

Use source-byte hashes plus original row keys; preserve plot/site/year, management, measurements, missingness, and source labels separately. For spatial data, inspect CRS before geometry transformations. A field-upload preview can demonstrate CSV and geometry mapping with Akron; the corn survey can demonstrate grouped repeat samples and honest unknowns. The Dryad N-rate and Transforming Drainage records can supply harder linked-table and intervention questions once sampled and schema-checked. Split evaluation by study/site/field and time, never by random row; check overlap among Ag Data Commons mirrors, KBS, LTAR, GRACEnet, and DRIVES before claiming disjoint populations. Field records support retrospective questions, not local prescription or Canadian authority.
